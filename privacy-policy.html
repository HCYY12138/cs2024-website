About this notice and personal data

This notice applies to personal data in our possession or under our control, including personal data held by organisations we engage to process data for our purposes.

A “customer” is an individual who contacts us to find out about goods or services we provide, or who may enter or has entered into a contract with us for the supply of goods or services.

“Personal data” means data, whether true or not, about a customer who can be identified from that data, or from that data together with other information to which we have or are likely to have access. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include your name, residential address, email address and telephone number. Other terms in this notice have the meanings given to them in the PDPA where the context permits.

Collection, use and disclosure

We generally collect personal data when you provide it voluntarily, directly or through a third party you have authorised, after you have been told why it is collected and have given written consent. We may also collect or use personal data without consent where the PDPA or other laws permit or require it. We will seek consent before collecting additional personal data or using it for a purpose you have not been notified about, except where the law permits or authorises otherwise.

We may collect and use your personal data to:

  • perform obligations connected with providing the goods or services you request; and
  • respond to, handle and process your queries, requests, applications, complaints and feedback.

We may disclose your personal data where disclosure is required to perform obligations connected with providing the goods or services you request.

Withdrawing your consent

Your consent remains valid until you withdraw it in writing. You may ask us to stop collecting, using or disclosing your personal data for any or all of the purposes in this notice by writing or emailing our Data Protection Officer using the contact details below.

We may need reasonable time to process your request, depending on its complexity and effect on our relationship with you. In general, we will seek to process it within five business days of receipt and notify you of the consequences, including any legal consequences that may affect your rights or liabilities. Depending on the nature and scope of your request, we may be unable to continue providing our goods or services; if so, we will notify you before completing the request. You may cancel your withdrawal request by writing to us.

Withdrawing consent does not affect our right to collect, use or disclose personal data without consent where applicable laws permit or require it.

Access to and correction of personal data

You may write or email our Data Protection Officer to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, information about how we use or disclose it, or a correction or update to your personal data.

A reasonable fee may be charged for an access request. If so, we will tell you the fee before processing your request. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible and generally within five business days. If we cannot respond within 30 days, we will tell you in writing within that period when we expect to respond. If we cannot provide data or make a requested correction, we will generally explain why, except where the PDPA does not require us to do so.

Protection and accuracy of personal data

We use appropriate administrative, physical and technical measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal and similar risks. These measures include minimising collection, authentication and access controls, need-to-know limits on disclosure, encryption and web security measures.

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While security cannot be guaranteed, we strive to protect your information and regularly review and enhance our security measures.

We generally rely on information you or your authorised representative provide. Please tell our Data Protection Officer in writing or by email if your personal data changes so we can keep it current, complete and accurate.

Retention of personal data

We may retain your personal data for as long as needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required or permitted by applicable laws. We will stop retaining it, or remove the means by which it can be associated with you, when it is reasonable to assume retention no longer serves that purpose and is no longer necessary for legal or business purposes.

Transfers outside Singapore

We generally do not transfer your personal data outside Singapore. If we do, we will obtain your consent and take steps to ensure it continues to receive a standard of protection at least comparable to that provided under the PDPA.

This notice applies together with any other notices, contractual clauses and consent clauses relating to our collection, use and disclosure of your personal data. We may revise it from time to time without prior notice. Please refer to the last-updated date above to see whether it has changed. Your continued use of our services constitutes acknowledgement and acceptance of changes.
