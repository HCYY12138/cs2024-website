01 / Product questions

Fit & comfort

Are earplugs comfortable for side sleepers?

Comfort depends on the earplug's outer profile, ear-tip shape, your ear, and your pillow. Somnus Sleep Earplugs have flat ends that sit flush with your ear, plus two tip types in four sizes. Follow the fit guide and allow a few nights to settle in, because what works for one side sleeper may feel different to another.

How do I choose an ear-tip size?

Start with size M, which comes fitted, then follow the fit guide. A tip should feel secure without painful pressure. All four sizes are included, so try them one at a time rather than assuming both ears need the same size.

Can I wear them through the whole night?

Yes, they are made for overnight wear. Keep them clean and let them dry fully between uses. If they cause pain or irritation, take them out and seek suitable advice rather than forcing the fit.

02 / Realistic expectations

Sound & use

Will earplugs block all sound?

No. Somnus Sleep Earplugs are passive and rated at 27 dB (SNR), so they soften noise rather than silence it. What you still hear depends on your fit and the sound around you. Loud snoring, for example, stays noticeable but much less intense.

Will I still hear my alarm or other important sounds?

That depends on the alarm's volume and your fit. Your alarm should still get through, but test it on your first night before relying on it. Do not assume an emergency alert or a person calling to you will always be heard.

Do these earplugs need charging?

No. Somnus Sleep Earplugs are passive, so there is no battery, charging or app.

How should I clean them?

Take the tips off, wipe the body and tips with a damp cloth, then let everything air-dry completely before reattaching them. Never submerge the tips, and avoid harsh chemicals and sharp objects.

Shipping & returns

Where can it be delivered?

Somnus Sleep Earplugs are sold through our Shopee Mall listing. For Singapore orders, check that your address is eligible on the listing before payment.

When will it arrive?

The seller and courier provide the live estimate at checkout. Confirm order cutoffs and tracking there, especially before a date-sensitive purchase.

What does shipping cost?

Fees, vouchers and free-shipping eligibility may change. Use the total shown by the marketplace at checkout rather than a fixed amount on this site.

Can I return the earplugs if the fit is not right?

Shopee Mall offers a 15-day return period from delivery, but Shopee's terms for change-of-mind returns require the item to be new and sealed, so a pair you have worn may not qualify. Read the listing's terms before ordering, and start any return through your Shopee order.

What if the item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Photograph the item and packaging, then use the marketplace's order-help process within its stated timeframe. Keep the order record and follow the seller's instructions.

Delivery estimates and return terms are confirmed on the Shopee Mall listing at checkout.
