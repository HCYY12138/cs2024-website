01 / Product questions

Fit & comfort

Are sleepbuds comfortable for side sleepers?

Comfort depends on the product's outer profile, ear-tip shape, your ear, and your pillow. Check the selected model's dimensions and tip options, then follow its fitting instructions. A model that works for one side sleeper may feel different to another.

How do I choose an ear-tip size?

Use the manufacturer's fitting guide for the exact product. A tip should feel secure without painful pressure. If several sizes are included, try them one at a time as directed rather than assuming both ears need the same size.

Can I wear them through the whole night?

Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for duration, cleaning and ear health. If the product causes pain or irritation, remove it and seek suitable advice rather than forcing the fit.

02 / Realistic expectations

Sound & use

Will sleepbuds block all sound?

No product should be presented as making a room silent. Passive models reduce some incoming sound; electronic models may play masking audio or offer other features. The experience depends on the exact model, fit and sound around you.

Will I still hear my alarm or other important sounds?

That varies with the product, sound level and fit. Test your alarm in a safe setting before relying on it. Do not assume an emergency alert or a person calling to you will always be heard.

Do these sleepbuds need charging?

Passive earplugs do not. Electronic sleepbuds do. Check the selected model's product details for its actual power, audio and charging specifications.

How should I clean them?

Follow the selected manufacturer’s care instructions. Materials and electronics differ, so do not submerge or wash a product unless its instructions specifically allow it.

04 / Before checkout

Shipping & returns

Where can it be delivered?

Availability and delivery areas depend on the selected seller. For Singapore orders, check that your address is eligible on the marketplace listing before payment.

When will it arrive?

The seller and courier provide the live estimate at checkout. Confirm order cutoffs and tracking there, especially before a date-sensitive purchase.

What does shipping cost?

Fees, vouchers and free-shipping eligibility may change. Use the total shown by the marketplace at checkout rather than a fixed amount on this site.

Can I return the sleepbuds if the fit is not right?

Return eligibility, time limits, hygiene exclusions and the condition required depend on the seller and marketplace policy for the actual listing. Read those terms before ordering, and contact the seller through the marketplace if you need to start a return.

What if the item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Photograph the item and packaging, then use the marketplace's order-help process within its stated timeframe. Keep the order record and follow the seller's instructions.
