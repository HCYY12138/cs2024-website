The technology behind it

How does it work?

  1. A closed loop that stays put

    The soft silicone loop settles into the shape of your ear, so it stays in when you toss and turn.

  2. Two layers in one tip

    The Foam-Silicone tip wraps memory foam in soft silicone: foam for the seal, silicone for the feel and to keep sweat out, so the seal holds on humid nights. Prefer firmer? Swap to the Double Tip.

  3. Flat, low-profile ends

    The ends sit flush with your ear, so nothing digs in when your head is on the pillow.

  4. 27 dB SNR

    Enough to take the edge off snoring, turn a roommate's late call into a murmur and soften cabin hum on a flight.
