Turns snoring down, not off

Loop Dream is not noise cancelling. It softens a snoring partner so it stops waking you, and you'll still hear your alarm.

  • Snoring becomes a low hum in the background.
  • Very loud sounds, like sirens, still get through at a lower volume.

How it works

Made to be slept on

Foam seals well but presses when you lie on it. Plain silicone is comfy but slips. Loop Dream is built to do both.

  1. A closed loop that stays put

    The soft silicone loop settles into the shape of your ear, so it stays in when you toss and turn.

  2. Two layers in one tip

    The oval tip wraps memory foam in soft silicone: foam for the seal, silicone for the feel. Prefer firmer? Swap to the double-stacked tip.

  3. Flat, low-profile ends

    The ends sit flush with your ear, so nothing digs in when your head is on the pillow.

  4. 27 dB SNR

    The highest noise reduction in Loop's range: enough to take the edge off snoring and street noise.

Before you buy

Will I still hear my alarm?

Loop Dream softens sound rather than blocking it, so most alarms still get through. Test yours on the first night to be sure.

What if none of the tips fit?

Try a different size in each ear, then the other tip type. Still no luck? Ask us for fit help. Shopee Mall also gives you free 15-day returns.

Why pay S$64.95 when foam costs under S$1?

Foam blocks a lot but can press and fall out when you sleep on your side, and you throw it away. Loop Dream is reusable and shaped to lie on.

Is it drug-free?

Yes. No pills, no battery, nothing to charge. Just soft silicone and foam.
