The technology behind it

How do Somnus Sleep Earplugs work?

A soft silicone loop holds the earplug in place, and a two-layer tip seals your ear canal. Foam alone presses when you lie on it. Plain silicone slips. Somnus is built to do both jobs.

  1. A closed loop that stays put

    The soft silicone loop settles into the shape of your ear, so it stays in when you toss and turn.

  2. Two layers in one tip

    The Foam-Silicone tip wraps memory foam in soft silicone: foam for the seal, silicone for the feel and to keep sweat out. Prefer firmer? Swap to the Double Tip.

  3. Flat, low-profile ends

    The ends sit flush with your ear, so nothing digs in when your head is on the pillow.

  4. 27 dB SNR

    Enough to make a fridge hum disappear, turn a TV next door into a faint murmur and take the edge off snoring.

Why do foam earplugs fail in Singapore's humidity?

Open foam absorbs sweat and moist air, so it softens and loses its seal. Somnus keeps the memory foam wrapped in silicone, so the tip holds its shape through a warm, humid night.

  • Fan-only bedrooms

  • Next to a snorer

    Soft snoring can fade out completely. Loud snoring stays noticeable, but much less intense.
