Our Story: The Search for Silence

We didn't invent sleepbuds. We just realized how desperately people need them to save their sleep—and their relationships.

Read our research journey ↓
The Problem We Found

The Noise That Ruins the Night

During our research, we discovered a hidden crisis in Singapore: 33% of adults struggle with poor sleep, and for 45% of women, the culprit is a partner's snoring. Nearly half of couples resort to sleeping in separate rooms.

In small HDB flats, escaping to another room isn't always an option. The market was flooded with cheap foam earplugs, but they either hurt or didn't block the deep rumble of snoring. We realized we needed a curated, transparent guide to better sleep solutions.

Why We Built This Site

Transparency Over Hype

We are a research team, not a manufacturer. We built this site because buying sleep products online is confusing. Prices fluctuate, and claims are often exaggerated.

We created Somnus as a transparent, curated space. We only feature the Loop Dream sleepbuds because our testing showed they actually deliver what they promise: 27dB of noise reduction and a pressure-free fit that helps you reclaim your Peace.

Behind the Scenes

How we evaluated our products.

01

Selection Criteria

We evaluate products based on three pillars: Comfort (can you sleep on your side?), Function (does it block low-frequency snoring?), and Value (is it worth the investment?).

02

What We Learned

Real testing and interviews taught us that physical noise reduction is only half the battle. The other half is the psychological relief of knowing you won't be woken up at 3 AM.

03

Our Promise to You

We don't invent fake reviews. We link directly to live marketplace listings (like Shopee) so you can see real-time prices, current deals, and verified seller terms.
